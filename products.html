Technical FAQ

Technical FAQ

Common questions from engineering and operations teams.

How do Robosync services integrate with existing ERP systems?

Robosync exposes REST and WebSocket connectors for SAP, Oracle, and Microsoft Dynamics, syncing production orders, inventory, and machine state in near real time.

What safety certifications do your robots hold?

All platforms are certified to ISO 10218 and ISO/TS 15066, with CE, UL, and functional-safety SIL-3 rated controllers on collaborative models.

Do you provide on-site installation and training?

Yes. Every deployment includes on-site commissioning and operator/technician certification training delivered at your facility or a regional platform center.

What is your average deployment timeline?

Typical single-cell deployments take 8–12 weeks from specification to production. Fleet-scale rollouts are phased and parallelized to minimize line downtime.

What SLAs do you offer?

We offer 99.5% uptime SLAs with 4-hour remote response and 24-hour on-site response in supported regions, plus hardware replacement guarantees.
